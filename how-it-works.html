How ScamSOS works

A guide for the first hour after an online scam: what to do, what to save, and who to send it to.

The three pieces

1. The app (index.html)Guides you through what to do for four kinds of scam, has a Call 1930 button, a calm-down screen, a voice assistant, and builds your report, bank letter and proof pack. It runs on your phone.
↓
2. Your account (account.html)Optional. Register, log in and save a report so you have a record and can see its status. The police desk lives here too.
↓
3. The database (Supabase)Stores accounts, saved reports and proof screenshots. Access rules decide who can see what.

What happens, step by step

1. A person opens the app and taps what happened, or taps Talk.
2. They get a short "do this now" list and can call 1930 with one tap.
3. They answer a few questions and attach proof. The app writes a summary and a bank letter.
4. They use the report (see below). If they want a record, they tap Save to my account, log in, and the report is stored.
5. A police officer with an approved account sees saved reports on the police desk, updates the status and can leave a note the person can read.

Where the report is used

PieceWhere it goes
SummaryRead out on the 1930 call, or copy into the complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Check the portal's current fields when you file.
Bank letterThe branch manager or the bank's grievance / nodal officer, by email, in person or in the bank's app. Ask for a complaint number.
Proof screenshotsAttach to the portal complaint and keep with the bank letter. The PDF includes them.
Saved copyYour account record. Useful if the bank does not reply and you escalate (for example to the RBI Ombudsman) or visit a police station.

Official places to report (real links)

WhereUse it for
1930 helpline
Call 1930		Money lost to online fraud. Available 24 hours. Call first.
cybercrime.gov.inThe national portal to file the full complaint, attach evidence and track it with your acknowledgement number.
Your bank's official app or the number on your cardBlock cards/UPI and raise a written dispute. Ask for a complaint number.
sancharsaathi.gov.in (Chakshu)Suspicious calls, SMS or WhatsApp, within 30 days. It does not handle money lost; use 1930 for that.
cms.rbi.org.in (RBI Ombudsman)Only after your bank has had 30 days to respond and has not fixed it. Free. Helpline 14448. Under the scheme effective 1 July 2026 you then have 90 days to file, so confirm the current rules on the site.

Always type these addresses yourself or use the links here. Do not trust numbers or links from search ads or from the scammer.

Good to know

This is an independent guide, not a government service. Saving a report here does not file a complaint. Always call 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in.

The AI voice conversation only runs inside Claude. On this website the voice assistant uses the simpler step-by-step flow.

On a shared phone, log out and clear the browser data after you finish.
